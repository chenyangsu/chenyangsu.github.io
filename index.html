I am an incoming Postdoctoral Scholar at Stanford University. I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Wisconsin–Madison working with Prof. Tianyuan Lu. I completed my PhD in Quantitative Life Sciences at McGill University (December 2025), advised by Prof. Sirui Zhou, where I built protein–disease atlases using proteogenomic approaches to identify causal proteins for complex diseases and nominate drug targets. Before that, I completed my MSc in Computer Science at McGill (2022), supervised by Prof. Joelle Pineau (Meta's former Global VP of AI) and Prof. Brent Richards (CEO of 5 Prime Sciences), affiliated with Mila – Quebec AI Institute and the Lady Davis Institute, and my BSc in Computer Science & Biology at McGill (2020).

My research is supported by a Postdoctoral Research Scholarship from the Fonds de recherche du Québec – Santé (FRQS) ($110,000 CAD; ranked 2nd of 15 applications in committee) and a Biswas Family Foundation Fast Grant ($25,000 USD) — seed funding from Sanjit Biswas's foundation for an AI-native human genetics knowledge base for causal target discovery; the inaugural program funded 37 of 685 applications worldwide (5.4%). My PhD was funded by a Canada Graduate Scholarship – Doctoral (CGS-D; $113,333 CAD), an FRQS Doctoral Training Fellowship ($96,000 CAD), and a Lady Davis Institute / TD Bank Studentship ($20,000 CAD) — over $450K CAD in total funding, alongside 56 additional honors and awards.

I have contributed to 52 publications (32 research articles and 20 correspondence articles) published or accepted in peer-reviewed venues including ICLR, The New England Journal of Medicine, Nature Genetics, Nature Metabolism, Nature Communications, The Lancet Haematology, European Heart Journal, JAMA, Diabetes Care, and Journal of Clinical Endocrinology and Metabolism. I have given invited talks at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard, Stanford University School of Medicine, Academia Sinica (Genomics Research Center, Taiwan), the National Taiwan University, and the Banff International Research Station.

I am a co-founder of a target discovery startup (in stealth), an elected member of the Young Investigators Committee of the International Genetic Epidemiology Society, and an investment fellow at HaloHealth. Previously, I was the first Canadian intern at Hinge Health (since IPO'd, raising $437.3M) and a world finalist in the $500K USD XPRIZE Pandemic Response Challenge.
