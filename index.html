Research

Three threads. The genetics work grounds the evaluation work: both ask how to tell a real causal signal from a convincing artefact.

AI Evals & Agents for Biomedicine

I build benchmarks and evaluation harnesses that test whether LLM agents are actually reliable on biomedical and clinical tasks — outcome-based grading against a simulated record, repeated trials, and cluster-bootstrap confidence intervals instead of a single headline score. It continues the reinforcement learning work I started on how agents behave under a learned reward.

Code ICLR 2021

Human Genetics & Proteomics for Target Discovery

Using Mendelian randomization and colocalization over plasma pQTLs, I separate proteins that plausibly cause disease from the far larger set that merely correlate with it, and turn the survivors into drug-target hypotheses. Most of my published work sits here, across osteoarthritis, type 2 diabetes and cardiometabolic disease.

Nat Commun paper Publications

AI-Native Scientific Knowledge Systems

I am building a human genetics knowledge base designed to be queried and reasoned over by agents, rather than read as static supplementary tables. The aim is that evidence for a causal target stays machine-checkable end to end, from variant to claim.
