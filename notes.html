Motivation

Calvin Coolidge
30th U.S. President
“Nothing in this world can take the place of persistence. Talent will not; nothing is more common than unsuccessful men with talent. Genius will not; unrewarded genius is almost a proverb. Education will not; the world is full of educated derelicts. Persistence and determination alone are omnipotent. The slogan ‘Press On’ has solved, and always will solve, the problems of the human race.” — Calvin Coolidge, as recited in The Founder (2016)
